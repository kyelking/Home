Why aluminum frames?

Zippers are the first thing to fail on a suitcase. Our cases close on an aluminum frame with locking clasps, so there's no zipper to split, snag or get forced open.

  • 01 Polycarbonate shell that flexes under impact and springs back.
  • 02 360° double spinner wheels for smooth, quiet rolling.
  • 03 TSA-approved locks built into the frame.
  • 04 Lined interior with compression straps and a divider.

Will the carry-on fit in the overhead bin?

The 20" cases measure about 55 × 37 × 23 cm including wheels, which fits most major US and EU airlines. Budget airlines often have smaller limits, so check yours before you fly.

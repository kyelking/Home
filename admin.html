$100k / month plan

ROAS 2–3 is typical for cold Meta/TikTok traffic on $150–$400 products once ads are dialed in. The first few weeks are usually worse while you test creatives.

Sourcing links

Search terms are pre-filled. Pick suppliers with 4.7★+, 500+ orders, a US warehouse option, and real customer photos. Order one sample of each before running ads.

Export to Shopify

Download a product CSV, then Shopify admin → Products → Import. Add photos and link each product to its supplier with DSers or CJ Dropshipping so orders are placed automatically.

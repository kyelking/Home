Sourcing links

On AliExpress, sort by Orders and look for: 4.7★+, 500+ sold, 50+ reviews with photos, a store open 2+ years, and a "Ships from United States" option. Copy the rating, reviews and sold count into supplier in products.js and this table checks them for you.

Sample quality checklist

Order one of each product to your home before you run ads. Drop any product that fails two or more checks.

  • Wheels roll quietly on tile and carpet, with no wobble
  • Handle extends smoothly, locks at every height, little rattle
  • Shell flexes and springs back with no white stress marks
  • Frame clasps or zippers close evenly and don't snag
  • TSA lock opens with the code and the TSA key slot
  • No chemical smell after 24 hours open
  • Leather: real grain, stitched edges, not peeling (Atelier)
  • Alloy: magnet doesn't stick, dents look clean (Monolith)
  • Lining is stitched, not glued; no loose threads
  • Box arrives undamaged. If not, ask for extra packaging
  • Carry-on measures ≤ 55 × 40 × 23 cm including wheels
  • Real weight is within 10% of the listing
